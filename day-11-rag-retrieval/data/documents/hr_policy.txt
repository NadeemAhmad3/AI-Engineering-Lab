# Human Resources & Leave Policy
The maximum annual leave allowance for full-time employees is 25 days per calendar year.
Employees must submit leave requests at least 5 business days in advance via the HR portal.
Parental leave provides up to 12 weeks of fully paid leave for eligible birth or adoptive parents.
Health insurance coverage takes effect on the first day of the month following employment start date.
Sick leave permits up to 10 paid days annually upon doctor notification.

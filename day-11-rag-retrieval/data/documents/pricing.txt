# Platform Pricing & Tier Structures
The Enterprise Tier subscription costs 499 USD per month and includes unlimited API predictions and 24/7 dedicated support.
The Standard Tier subscription costs 49 USD per month with a quota of 100,000 API calls.
Free Tier includes up to 1,000 API inferences per month at zero cost.
Volume discounts apply for annual commitments exceeding 10 million API predictions.
Overage billing is charged at 0.0005 USD per additional request.
